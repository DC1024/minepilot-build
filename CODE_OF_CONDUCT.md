# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MinePilot contributors, maintainers, testers, and community members commit to
making participation safe and welcoming regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression,
experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance,
race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act in the interest of an open, respectful, and technically rigorous
community. Disagreement about evidence or design is welcome; personal attacks,
harassment, and retaliation are not.

## Expected behavior

Examples of constructive participation include:

- using respectful and inclusive language;
- giving specific, evidence-based technical feedback;
- accepting responsibility, correcting mistakes, and helping repair harm;
- respecting privacy, consent, world ownership, and security boundaries;
- keeping criticism focused on code, behavior, documentation, or evidence; and
- prioritizing community safety when product goals conflict with it.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, or attention;
- insults, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption;
- discriminatory jokes or demeaning comments;
- publishing private information, credentials, logs, identities, or world data
  without explicit permission;
- pressuring anyone to disclose private correspondence or test material;
- knowingly making false claims about another contributor or the project's
  verified capabilities; and
- retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
commits, project-operated chat, testing sessions, and public spaces where a
person is representing MinePilot. It also applies to private interactions when
they directly affect a person's ability to participate safely in the project.

## Reporting

Report a conduct concern confidentially through the repository's **Security**
tab by selecting **Report a vulnerability**. Start the title with
`[CODE OF CONDUCT]`; this route is used only to keep the report private and
does not imply that the incident is a software vulnerability.

Include, when safe to do so, the date, location, people involved, a factual
description, relevant links or screenshots, witnesses, and any immediate
safety or privacy needs. Do not post private incident details in a public
issue. If private reporting is not available, open a public issue titled
`Request confidential conduct contact` without incident details. A maintainer
will provide a private reporting route.

Maintainers will acknowledge a report within seven calendar days when the
platform permits contact, limit access to people needed for review, avoid
unnecessary disclosure, and communicate the outcome when doing so is safe.
A maintainer named in a report must not investigate or decide that report.
Reports made in good faith will not result in retaliation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, request a correction or apology, issue a
warning, restrict participation temporarily, or permanently ban a participant.
The response will be proportional to the behavior, its impact, prior conduct,
and any continuing risk. Illegal activity or a credible threat may be referred
to the relevant platform or authorities when necessary to protect people.

Enforcement decisions should record the rule applied and the action taken while
preserving reporter and affected-person privacy. A person subject to an action
may request one reconsideration through the same private reporting route. A
maintainer who did not make the original decision should review it when one is
available.
